BDO Picker guide

Pick your BDO dates in Telegram. First come, first served, with a queue behind whoever picks first.

For everyone

How to pick, swap out and keep track of your BDO days.

1. Open it

Tap the BDO Picker link pinned in the group chat. It opens inside Telegram. There's no login: Telegram tells it who you are.

Opening it in a normal browser only shows "Open BDO Picker from the Telegram link." Always use the Telegram link.

2. First time only: link your callsign

  1. Pick your callsign from the list. Callsigns that are greyed out are already linked to someone.
  2. Tap I'm [your callsign].
  3. When Telegram asks whether the bot may message you, tap Allow. That's how you'll hear when you move up to BDO.

Callsign missing, or someone took yours? Message the BDO planner.

3. Read the calendar

Open. A BDO day nobody has picked yet.
5HAWK
Taken. Someone is BDO. You can still join the queue.
13BDO
Yours. You're BDO, or you're in the queue (shows QUEUE 2 and so on).
6 
Plain number. Not a BDO day, so it can't be tapped.

Use the ‹ › arrows at the top to change month. Tap any coloured day to see who is BDO and who is queuing.

4. Pick a day

  1. Tap the day.
  2. Tap Take this day if it's open: you're the BDO.
  3. If someone got there first, tap Join the queue. You're next in line after the people already queuing.

While it saves, the day shows SAVING… for a second or two. Pick as many days as you like. My days at the bottom lists everything you hold.

5. Can't make it?

If you're the BDO: tap the day, then Drop this day. Number 1 in the queue becomes BDO and gets a Telegram message. If nobody is queuing, the day goes back to open.

BDOHAWK dropped
1VIPER → now BDO, gets a message
2OSPREY → now number 1

If you're in the queue: tap the day, then Leave the queue. Everyone behind you moves up one place.

Good to know

  • Past days can't be picked or dropped.
  • The app shows the picks as they were when you opened it. Close and reopen it to see the latest.
  • Not getting bot messages? Open the BDO Picker bot's chat once and tap Start.

For planners

Admins see a Planner tab next to Pick days.

1. Set the BDO days

  1. Open the Planner tab and go to the month with ‹ ›.
  2. Tap a date to make it a BDO day. It turns blue and shows OPEN to everyone straight away.
  3. Tap it again to remove it. If someone has already picked it, the app asks first, and everyone on that day gets a Telegram message that it's no longer needed.
Crew can only pick days you have set. Post in the group once a month's days are in.

2. See who has what

  • The three counts at the top: BDO days, have a BDO and still open (in red) for the month on screen.
  • On the calendar, BDO +2 means a BDO plus two people queuing. OPEN means nobody yet.
  • Who has what lists every day with its BDO and the queue in order.

3. Pass on or share planner rights

  1. Scroll to Admins at the bottom of the Planner tab.
  2. Pick a callsign and tap Add admin. They get a Telegram message. The person must have opened BDO Picker once first.
  3. To hand over, add the new planner, then tap Remove me next to your own name.

You can only remove yourself, not other admins. The last admin can't leave, so the app is never left without one.

4. Fixing things in the Google Sheet

Everything lives in the BDO Picker Google Sheet. The Sheet owner can fix anything by hand:

  • Crew tab: add or remove callsigns in column A. To unlink someone who picked the wrong callsign, clear their Telegram ID and username (columns B and C). They pick again next time they open the app.
  • Admins tab: one callsign per row. Use this if you're ever locked out.
  • Days tab: one row per BDO date: the date (YYYY-MM-DD), the BDO, and the queue (callsigns separated by commas, in order).
  • Log tab: every pick, drop, day change and admin change, with who and when. "message to X not delivered" means that person needs to tap Start in the bot's chat.
